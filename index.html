About Me Details

Hi! I’m Muhammad Rafi Andika, a junior software developer with a strong passion for building intuitive and high-performance digital products. With over 10 projects under my belt, I specialize in creating seamless experiences using modern tools and frameworks such as Tailwind CSS, Laravel, Flutter, and React.js. I take pride in crafting clean, responsive interfaces and scalable applications that cater to users’ needs. My journey has led me to explore both front-end and back-end development, always aiming to find the perfect balance between functionality and aesthetics. I thrive in dynamic environments where I can continuously learn and grow, staying up-to-date with the latest tech innovations. Along the way, I’ve had the opportunity to attend seminars organized by prominent tech communities like Dicoding and GDG (Google Developer Groups), including events hosted by GDG Bogor and GDG Surabaya. These experiences have not only expanded my knowledge but have also connected me with like-minded developers and industry experts. Whether it’s designing beautiful UI/UX or developing powerful back-end solutions, I’m always eager to take on new challenges and contribute to impactful projects. Let’s connect and collaborate to build something great!
